Biography

A commitment to serving the State and its citizens

Secuna Baldé is a politician, environmentalist and civil rights advocate of the Republic of Guinea-Bissau. His career combines government responsibilities, a parliamentary mandate, a diplomatic career and an unwavering commitment to sustainable development.

A former Minister of Tourism and Handicrafts and Member of the National People's Assembly, he also served as adviser to the Office of the President of the Republic, adviser to the Prime Minister for Trade and Investment, and adviser to the Minister of National Defence and of the Freedom Fighters of the Homeland.

He was also a non-permanent member of the National Elections Commission (CNE) and currently works as a consultant in investment and project development in the tourism and hospitality sectors.

Vision for the country

A prosperous, green and united Guinea-Bissau

Four pillars guide Secuna Baldé's public commitment, in the service of all Bissau-Guineans.

🏨

Tourism, handicrafts and the economy

Making sustainable tourism and handicrafts engines of employment and national wealth, showcasing the Bijagós archipelago and attracting responsible investment.

🌍

Delivering the "Green Guinea-Bissau 2030" vision: protecting forests, coastlines and biodiversity, and supporting a circular economy that serves communities.

Political career

An early and determined political career

January 2014

President of the Socialist Party (PS) of Guinea-Bissau

Elected party president at the 1st Congress of this political formation at the age of 32, he became the youngest political party leader in the country.

2014 general elections

Head of list and candidate for Prime Minister

In the 2014 general elections, he stood as head of list and candidate for the office of Prime Minister under the colours of the Socialist Party (PS) of Guinea-Bissau.

MADEM-G15

Movement for Democratic Alternation — Group of 15

A former activist and founding member of MADEM-G15, he notably served as:

  • Former under-secretary of the Bureau of External Relations
  • Member of the National Political Commission

PTG

Guinean Workers' Party

Within the PTG, he served as:

  • Activist
  • Former sixth vice-president
  • Spokesperson
  • Secretary for External Relations
  • Member of the Standing Committee
